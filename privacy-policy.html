Privacy Policy — Wi-Fi Signal M3E

Effective Date: November 20, 2026

Developer: Matt White (mattwhite794@gmail.com)

Summary: Wi-Fi Signal M3E is designed with privacy first. IP addresses and router MAC (BSSID) addresses are never shared publicly, and you can use Anonymous Login Mode at any time.

1. Information We Access on Your Device

  • Location (Fine / Coarse & Nearby Wi-Fi Devices): Used only while the app is active to read your Wi-Fi network name (SSID) as required by Android OS and to pin your speed tests on the GPS Signal Map.
  • Phone / SIM State (Optional): Used only to detect which SIM slot (SIM 1, SIM 2, or eSIM) is actively handling mobile data.
  • Notifications (Optional): Used to send Poor Wi-Fi alerts when your signal degrades.

2. Google Account Sign-In & Cloud Sync (Optional)

You may optionally sign in with your Google Account to sync your speed test history across devices and post to the Community Leaderboard or Chat. If you enable Anonymous Login Mode, your account details, SSID, SIM info, and IP/MAC details are hidden and locked.

3. Community Privacy Safeguards

Any IPv4, IPv6, or MAC/BSSID addresses are automatically stripped from public community posts and leaderboard entries. Only the signed-in device owner can view their own local IP or router BSSID on their own phone.

4. AI Wi-Fi Assistant

The built-in On-Device Advisor runs locally on your phone. If you choose to enter your own optional Google Gemini API key, your questions and live Wi-Fi telemetry are sent directly to Google's Gemini API under your own API key.

5. Data Deletion & Contact

You can delete your AI chat logs, GPS map pins, and community posts/comments directly inside the app at any time. For any privacy questions, contact mattwhite794@gmail.com.
