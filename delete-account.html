How Account & Data Deletion Works:
Wi-Fi Signal M3E uses your Google Account solely to display your name and sync your speed test history across your devices. You can delete your data directly inside the app or request complete deletion using the email link below.

Option 1: Delete Your Data Inside the App

  1. Open Wi-Fi Signal M3E on your Android device.
  2. Go to Settings → Google, Account & Privacy (or tap the Leaderboard / Social Chat icon at the top) and tap Sign Out to unlink your Google Account from the app.
  3. To delete community posts, comments, or leaderboard entries you posted, tap the Delete (trash) icon on any of your posts or comments.
  4. To delete your AI Assistant chat logs, open the AI Wi-Fi Assistant → History → Clear All.
  5. To delete GPS Signal Map pins, open the Signal Map tab and delete individual pins or tap Clear All.

Option 2: Request Full Cloud Account Deletion by Email

What Data Is Deleted vs. Retained

  • Deleted immediately / within 7 days: Your synced cloud speed test history, Google Account profile link, and any community posts/comments you request to remove.
  • Anonymous Mode: If you use Anonymous Login Mode, no personal Google Account email or device identifiers are ever stored.
